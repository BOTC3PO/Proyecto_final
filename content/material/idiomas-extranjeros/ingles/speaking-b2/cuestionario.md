  - "Identificar la estructura para expresar acuerdo con matices."
  - "Revisar el uso de 'I agree, but...' en contexto."

  - "However"
  - "But"
tipo: completar

enunciado: "I think renewable energy is important for the environment. ___ I believe it should also be affordable for everyone."
