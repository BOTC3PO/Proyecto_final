## Introducción
El **past simple** cuenta acciones terminadas en un momento del pasado: *I **visited** Salta last year.* Los verbos **regulares** agregan *-ed*; los **irregulares** tienen una forma propia que hay que aprender.

## Explicación central
### Verbos regulares: + *-ed*
- *work → worked*, *play → played*, *watch → watched*.
- Terminados en *-e*: solo *-d*: *live → lived*, *dance → danced*.
- Consonante + *y*: *-ied*: *study → studied*, *try → tried* (pero *play → played*).
- Consonante-vocal-consonante con acento al final: se duplica: *stop → stopped*, *plan → planned*.
- Pronunciación de *-ed*: /t/ (*worked*), /d/ (*played*), /ɪd/ después de t o d (*wanted*, *needed*).

### Verbos irregulares frecuentes
*be → was/were, go → went, have → had, do → did, make → made, get → got, see → saw, come → came, take → took, eat → ate, drink → drank, buy → bought, think → thought, write → wrote, say → said, give → gave, find → found, leave → left, know → knew, meet → met.*

### Negativa y pregunta: con *did*
- **Negativa**: *didn't + verbo en forma base*: *I **didn't go** to school.* (❌ *didn't went*)
- **Pregunta**: ***Did** you **see** the match?* — *Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.*
- **Con palabra interrogativa**: *Where **did** you **go**?*
- **Excepción, *to be***: *I wasn't there. Were you tired?* (sin *did*)

### Expresiones de tiempo
*yesterday, last night/week/year, two days ago, in 2010, when I was a child.*

## Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
1. **Pasado después de *did***: ❌ *Did you went?* ❌ *I didn't saw it.* ✅ *Did you go? I didn't see it.*
2. **Regularizar irregulares**: ❌ *goed, buyed, eated* ✅ *went, bought, ate*.
3. **Usar *did* con *be***: ❌ *I didn't be tired.* ✅ *I wasn't tired.*
4. **Pronunciar la *e* de -ed siempre**: *worked* suena /wɜːkt/, no «work-ed».

## Comparación con el español
El *past simple* corresponde al pretérito perfecto simple («fui, comí, vi») y muchas veces también al imperfecto narrativo. A diferencia del español, la forma es igual para todas las personas: *I went, she went, they went*.

## Ejemplo extendido en contexto
*Last summer we **went** to Jujuy. We **stayed** in Purmamarca and **visited** the Hill of Seven Colours. I **took** hundreds of photos. We **didn't have** much time, but we **ate** amazing empanadas. **Did** you ever **go** there?*
