# **There is / There are: Afirmativa, negativa e interrogativa (con some/any)**

## Introducción
**There is / there are** equivalen a «hay»: sirven para decir que algo **existe** o **está** en un lugar: *There is a bank near here.*

## Explicación central
### Forma
- **Singular e incontables**: ***There is** (there's) a book on the table. **There is** some milk in the fridge.*
- **Plural**: ***There are** two cats in the garden.*
- **Negativa**: *There **isn't** a bank here. There **aren't** any chairs.*
- **Pregunta**: ***Is there** a pharmacy near here? — Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.* ***Are there** any eggs? — Yes, there are.*

### some / any / a / no
- *a/an* con singular contable: *There's **a** dog.*
- **some** en afirmativas (plural o incontable): *There are **some** apples. There's **some** water.*
- **any** en negativas y preguntas: *There aren't **any** apples. Is there **any** water?*
- *There's **no** milk* = *There isn't **any** milk.*

### Cuántos
*How many* + plural: ***How many** students **are there**?* — *How much* + incontable: ***How much** sugar **is there**?*

### En pasado y futuro
*There **was** / There **were** / There **will be**: *There **were** many people at the concert. There **will be** a meeting tomorrow.*

### There vs. it / they
- *There is a car outside.* (presenta algo nuevo) — ***It** is red.* (habla de algo ya mencionado)

## Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
1. ❌ *Have a bank near here.* ✅ *There is a bank near here.*
2. ❌ *There is two cats.* ✅ *There are two cats.*
3. ❌ *There aren't some chairs.* ✅ *There aren't any chairs.*
4. ❌ *Is a pharmacy here?* ✅ *Is there a pharmacy here?*

## Comparación con el español
«Hay» no cambia («hay un libro», «hay dos libros»), pero *there is/are* concuerda con el sustantivo que sigue. Además, «había» = *there was/were* y «habrá» = *there will be*.

## Ejemplo extendido en contexto
*In my neighbourhood **there is** a big park and **there are** three cafés. **There isn't** a cinema, but **there's** a library. **Are there any** good restaurants? Yes, **there are** some!*
