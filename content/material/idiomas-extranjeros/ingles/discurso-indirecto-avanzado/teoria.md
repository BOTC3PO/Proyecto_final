## Introducción
En el nivel B2 el discurso indirecto se amplía a **preguntas**, **órdenes y pedidos**, **modales** y a una variedad de **verbos introductorios** que resumen la intención del hablante (*suggest, advise, refuse, promise…*).

## Explicación central
### Preguntas indirectas
- **Preguntas cerradas** (sí/no): **if / whether** + orden afirmativo: *"Are you coming?"* → *She asked **if I was** coming.*
- **Preguntas abiertas**: **palabra interrogativa** + orden afirmativo, **sin *do/did***: *"Where do you live?"* → *He asked me **where I lived**.* (❌ *where did I live*)
- No llevan signo de pregunta: *She asked what time it was.*

### Órdenes y pedidos
- **tell / ask / order + persona + (not) to + infinitivo**: *"Close the door."* → *He **told me to close** the door.* — *"Don't be late."* → *She **told us not to be** late.* — *"Could you help me?"* → *She **asked me to help** her.*

### Modales
- *will → would, can → could, may → might, must → had to* (obligación) o *must* (deducción, no cambia).
- *Would, could, might, should, ought to* **no cambian**: *"You should rest."* → *He said I should rest.*

### Verbos introductorios con patrones
| Verbo | Patrón | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| advise, warn, remind, invite, encourage | + persona + to-inf | *She **advised me to see** a doctor.* |
| offer, promise, refuse, agree, threaten | + to-inf | *He **refused to pay**.* |
| suggest, recommend | + -ing / + that + sujeto + (should) verbo | *She **suggested going** out. / She suggested that we (should) go out.* |
| apologise for, insist on, admit, deny | + -ing | *He **apologised for being** late. She **denied taking** it.* |
| accuse … of, blame … for | + persona + of/for + -ing | *They **accused him of lying**.* |

## Errores comunes de hispanohablantes
1. Mantener el orden de pregunta: ❌ *She asked where did I live.* ✅ *She asked where I lived.*
2. ❌ *He suggested me to go.* ✅ *He suggested (that) I go / I should go.* o *He suggested going.*
3. ❌ *She said me to sit.* ✅ *She told me to sit.*

## Comparación con el español
«Me preguntó **si** venía» = *She asked **if** I was coming*; «Me dijo **que** cerrara la puerta» (subjuntivo) = *He told me **to close** the door* (infinitivo). El inglés no usa subjuntivo para las órdenes indirectas: usa *to* + infinitivo.

## Ejemplo extendido en contexto
*"Can you send me the report? And don't forget the graphs. Why did you miss the meeting?"*
→ *My boss **asked me to send** her the report and **reminded me not to forget** the graphs. Then she **asked why I had missed** the meeting. I **apologised for missing** it and **promised to be** on time next week.*
