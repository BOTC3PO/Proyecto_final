### 1 — Main Idea Identification

```
metadata:
  materia: "ingles"
  tema: "listening-b2"
  nivel: "B2"
  tags: ["main-idea", "lecture"]

pasos:
  - "Escucha el extracto de una conferencia sobre tecnología."
  - "Identifica la idea principal del discurso."

opciones_explicitas:
  - "the development of solar panels"
  - "the reduction of carbon emissions"
  - "the integration of wind and hydro power"
  - "the discovery of new battery storage methods"
respuesta: "the development of solar panels"
tipo: mc

enunciado: "Speaker: 'Of all the innovations we've discussed, it's really the development of solar panels that stands out as the single most significant leap forward for renewable energy.' The speaker emphasizes that the most significant advancement in renewable energy is..."

explicacion: "La idea principal se centra en la innovación más impactante mencionada, que es el avance en paneles solares. Las otras opciones son detalles o alternativas menos enfatizadas."
```
