### 1 — Present Perfect with 'already'

```

  materia: "ingles"
  tema: "present-perfect"
  nivel: "B1"
  tags: ["already", "past-simple"]

pasos:
  - "Identifica el uso correcto de 'has already gone' o 'went'."
  - "Elige la opción que completa la oración correctamente."

  - "She has already went to the store."
  - "She has already gone to the store."
  - "She already went to the store."
respuesta: "She has already gone to the store."
tipo: mc

enunciado: "Which sentence uses 'already' with the present perfect correctly?"

explicacion: "El presente perfecto se usa para acciones recientes con 'already', requiriendo el verbo en particípio (gone)."
```
